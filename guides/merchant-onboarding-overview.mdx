The PayGood merchant onboarding flow lets you bring new merchants onto the platform. Each merchant goes through a Know Your Business (KYB) application before they can process live payments. Once approved, the merchant is activated and ready to accept payments through PayGood.

## Onboarding stages

Work through each stage in order. Later steps depend on the application `id` returned when you create the draft.

1. **Create a draft application** — Open a new application for the merchant and receive an application ID.
2. **Fill in the business profile** — Provide legal name, business type, email, and registered address.
3. **Add beneficial owners** — Supply identity information for every individual who owns more than 25% of the business.
4. **Add payout accounts** — Link the bank account where PayGood will settle funds.
5. **Provide underwriting data** — Share estimated processing volumes and a description of the merchant's business.
6. **Submit for review** — Send the completed application to PayGood's underwriting team.
7. **Approval and activation** — Once approved, the merchant account is activated and live payment processing can begin.

## What you'll need

Gather the following information before starting the application:

- Business legal name and any doing-business-as (DBA) name
- Business type (e.g., corporation, LLC, sole proprietorship)
- Registered business address
- Business tax ID, vaulted as a token via Basis Theory
- Beneficial owner details: full name, date of birth, personal address, and ownership percentage for each owner with more than 25% stake
- Payout bank account details, vaulted as a token via Basis Theory
- Estimated annual card processing volume and maximum transaction amount
- Estimated card volume distribution (card-present, e-commerce, mail order/telephone)

## Supported countries

PayGood currently supports merchant onboarding in the following countries:

| Country | Code |
|---------|------|
| United States | `US` |
| Canada | `CA` |

Country codes follow the [ISO 3166-1 alpha-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_3166-1_alpha-2) standard. Pass the appropriate code in the `country` field when creating a draft application.

<Note>
  Sensitive data like tax IDs and bank account numbers must be tokenized before sending to PayGood. Use [Basis Theory](https://basistheory.com) to vault these fields and pass the resulting token object — `{"id": "tok_...", "provider": "basistheory"}` — in place of the raw value.
</Note>
